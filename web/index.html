BACKGROUND COLLECTION

Keep your workspace up to date.

Settings are saved on this computer. Collection continues while the application is running, even when the browser is closed.

What is collected

GeM public bids and reverse auctions; CPPP, Defproc and HSL homepage tenders and corrigenda. CPPP includes both eProcurement and ePublishing. Homepage windows are limited; awards, cancellations and complete historical coverage are not yet collected.

Storage & access

SQLite stores notices, revisions and bookmarks locally. This is a single-workspace application bound to localhost. Closing a browser tab does not stop collection. A sleeping or powered-off computer cannot collect updates.
